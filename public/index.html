          How to play

          1. Create or join a room. Share the 5-letter room code (or invite link) with friends. Playing solo? Add Space Bots in the lobby.
          2. Take turns drawing. The drawer picks one of three secret space objects and has 60 seconds to sketch it. No letters or words!
          3. Guess in the chat. Everyone else types guesses. Correct answers stay hidden from players still guessing.
          4. Unlock clues. As time ticks, mission clues appear: category, a physics fact (like gravity vs. Earth’s 9.8 m/s²), a blurred real NASA image, and letters of the answer.
          5. Score points. Correct guess = +50 plus up to +50 speed bonus. The drawer earns +20 for every player who guesses.
          6. Learn the sky. After each round a NASA information card explains the object and how to observe it.

          Guess spam is blocked: wait a moment between guesses and don't repeat the same guess.
